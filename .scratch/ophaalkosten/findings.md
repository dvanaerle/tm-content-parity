## Summary

The Dutch site contains four HTML pages and one PDF that explicitly use `ophaalkosten`. One additional page describes a relevant geographic restriction without using that word.

The generally stated charge is **EUR 99 plus the standard delivery tariff**. The consumer terms are the only source that caps the combined amount, at **EUR 199**.

## Content differences to resolve

1. **Calculation versus deduction**

   The FAQ says the charge is calculated automatically "tijdens het afrekenen." The return form says it is deducted from the refund.

2. **Maximum charge appears only in the PDF**

   The consumer terms cap the combined pickup and delivery charge at EUR 199. The HTML pages do not mention this maximum.

3. **Terminology varies**

   The pages alternate between `standaard bezorgtarief`, `reguliere bezorgkosten`, and `reguliere bezorgtarief` for the amount added to the EUR 99 pickup charge.

4. **Incomplete standalone explanation**

   The general withdrawal page mentions deduction of pickup costs but does not state the amount or link the wording directly to the EUR 99 charge.

5. **Availability restriction is separated from pricing**

   The delivery-area page limits pickup to addresses inside Tuinmaximaal's delivery area, while the pricing pages do not state that restriction.

## Discovery scope

The sitemap contained 4,648 distinct `tuinmaximaal.nl` URLs at the time of checking. The review filtered sitemap routes related to FAQs, returns, withdrawal, pickup, delivery, cancellation, exchange, and terms. Those pages were checked for `ophaalkosten` and related wording including `ophaalservice`, pickup-cost descriptions, and EUR 99 references. Site-index searches were also used to identify the linked consumer-terms PDF.
