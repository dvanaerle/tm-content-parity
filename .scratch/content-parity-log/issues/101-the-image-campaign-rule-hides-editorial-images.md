# 101 — The image campaign rule hides editorial images

Type: bug
Status: ready-for-agent
Blocked by: None — can start immediately.
Parent: ../map.md

**What to build:** `IMAGE_CAMPAIGN` in `compare/images.mjs` stops matching `deal` inside
`ideale`, `ideal` and `wero`, and stops matching `actie` inside `actie-updates`. Today the
rule matches **nothing but editorial images**: on the corpus on disk every one of its 29
findings is collateral, because the artwork it was written for now leaves at extraction.

## Why the question exists

Ticket [89](89-what-a-one-sided-campaign-rule-would-catch.md) measured the campaign
patterns and found this on the way past. `IMAGE_CAMPAIGN` fires when either side matches,
which is correct for images — an image identity is a filename, and under a both-sides rule
production's campaign artwork would have been the largest single source of findings in the
dataset. **The rule is right. The pattern has no word boundary.**

89 reported 530 `image-campaign` findings of which 24 were collateral, "4.9%". **Those
numbers describe a corpus that no longer exists** — 446 of its 816 extracts were crawled
before ticket 64's region entry landed and still carried the banner. They are kept here
only as the origin of the ticket. The measurement that governs the work is in `## Comments`
below: on the current corpus the rule's output is **29 findings, 100% collateral**.

The text pattern's guard is `actie(?!f)`, one character rather than a boundary. 89 found
`interactieve` slips past it. Any change here needs the same care in the other direction:
the near-miss list is in 89 §6 and it is long.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** bug
**Summary:** the campaign image pattern matches sub-word fragments of editorial filenames,
so it hides real image findings in a `diagnostic` class where nobody reads them. What it
hides today is a customer-service contact block, a blog photo, a reviews-page image and a
payment-method logo — see `### Where these files actually are` for the paths and the pages.
**None of them has ever been campaign artwork**, and the pattern is the only reason they were
ever called it.

**Current behaviour.** `compare/images.mjs:25`:

```js
export const IMAGE_CAMPAIGN = /korting|actie(?!f)|deal|aanbieding|black[-_]?friday|sale/i;
```

Tested against the image key — the basename — at `images.mjs:64` (production-only image →
`image-campaign` instead of `image-missing`) and `:107` (new-only → `image-campaign` instead
of `image-added`). `compare/vocabulary.mjs:202-206` gives the class
`visibility: 'diagnostic'`, which `web/src/components/Ledger.jsx:120` hides behind the noise
toggle and `web/src/lib/reports.mjs:144` keeps out of every denominator. So the rule's
mistakes are invisible by construction, which is why 29 of 29 went unnoticed.

**Desired behaviour.** The pattern matches a campaign word when it is a *word* in the
filename, not when it is a run of letters inside another word. On the corpus on disk it
therefore matches nothing, and `image-campaign` reads 0 — which is the correct output for a
corpus that contains no campaign artwork.

**Key interfaces.** One export changes. The shape measured to work, offered as the
recommendation rather than as the only answer:

```js
export const IMAGE_CAMPAIGN =
  /korting|aanbieding|black[-_]?friday|(?<![a-z])deals?(?![a-z])|(?<![a-z])sales?(?![a-z])/i;
```

The `s?` matters: `sales_uk.png` and `deals-overzicht.jpg` are campaign filenames and a
boundary without the plural drops them. Lookbehind runs — `package.json` sets
`engines.node >= 22.12.0` — but **it would be the first in the repo**: nothing under
`compare/`, `shared/`, `crawl/` or `web/src` uses one today. If that is judged too clever
for a filename check, `(^|[^a-z])deals?([^a-z]|$)` is equivalent for `.test`, and the
choice is yours to make and to write down.

The doc comment at `images.mjs:15-24` still explains one-sidedness and is still correct.
Extend it with the second reason the rule now has: the banner leaves at extraction, so this
rule is no longer the mechanism that removes campaign artwork — it is the net for artwork
placed outside the banner. Do not delete the rule.

**Measured effect on the corpus on disk**, to be re-derived and recorded, not carried:

| class | before | after | note |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| `image-campaign` | 29 | **0** | diagnostic → the class empties |

| `image-missing` | 4,387 | **4,390** | +3 `actie-updates_*`, **work** — nl 1, be 1, uk 1 |

35 key occurrences stop matching; **0 start**. Nothing else moves.

- [ ] The existing test at `compare/compare.test.mjs:1254-1266` passes unchanged — its

**Traps.**

- **Dropping the `actie` arm has a named cost, and it is not zero.** A future campaign image
  called `zomeractie-2027.jpg`, carrying no `korting`, `sale`, `deal`, `aanbieding` or
  `black friday`, would land as `image-missing` on every page it sits on. That is accepted
  here because `#campaign-banner` is the primary mechanism (ADR 0003, 89 §7) and no regex
  distinguishes `actie-updates` from `zomer-actie` without naming a filename in code, which
  ticket 90 forbade. **Write this trade-off into the comment, do not leave it implicit.**
- **Do not name a filename, a campaign, a month or a year in the pattern.** Ticket 90's
  constraint holds here, and `crawl/extract.test.mjs:919-926` is the shape of test that
  enforces it.
- **The class is diagnostic, so no dashboard number moves except the noise count.** The only
  evidence this ticket can produce is the counts and the key list. Produce both.
- **89's near-miss list is the input, not a starting point to re-derive**: `winactie` 38,
  `ideale` 31, `ideal` 27, `saleté` 10, `idealisierend` 6, `idealen` 5, `antractiet` 2,
  `interactieve` 2, `wholesale` 1, `dealing` 1. A candidate pattern that matches any of them
  is not the fix.
- **The text pattern `PROMO` at `compare/text.mjs:25` is out of scope.** It is both-sides by
  design and 89 refused importing this rule's shape into it. A stale copy of it lives at
  `crawl/probes/prototype-parity-data.mjs:258`; leave it alone.

**Out of scope.**

- **Deleting `IMAGE_CAMPAIGN`.** Triaged and refused: the exclusion covers today's
  placement, and artwork dropped into page content outside `#campaign-banner` would return
  a page-count flood of `image-missing`. The rule is a cheap net; keep it.
- **The visibility of `image-campaign`.** It stays `diagnostic`. Ticket 75 decided that and
  this ticket is about what enters the class, not who reads it.
- **Adding the other five stores' campaign vocabulary** — `rabatt`, `angebot`, `aktion`,
  `réduction`, `promotion`, `discount`, `offre`. Out of scope here because it would make the
  "nothing else moves" criterion unmeasurable, and **the question is now closed rather than
  deferred**: [126](126-the-campaign-rule-and-the-other-five-stores.md) answered it
  2026-08-14 and the answer is **add none**. The reason is not that the corpus is empty. The
  campaign artwork this project recorded was named `kortingactie` in **all six stores** —
  `2026-07-23-kortingactie-{nl,de,fr,en}-16aug.svg`, where the suffix is a language tag and
  the campaign word is Dutch every time — so `korting` catches 4 of 4 and every foreign word
  catches **0 of 4, in its own store**. Filenames on this site are not translated even
  though the copy is, which is why ADR 0003's objection to a Dutch anchor does not carry
  from the text side to the image side. 126 §1. **Do not re-open this from the vocabulary
  side.** If it re-opens, it is because artwork appeared under a translated filename, and
  that is a new measurement and not this bullet.
